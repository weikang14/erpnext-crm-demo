Fixtures, texts (T1, T0, T403, TU), the sending claim and call names (c1-c5) are the same as in `test-cases.md`. The Layer column
uses the same codes (U, C, E, M). In short: D1 = siti's deal on TEC with Q133, 3 × FG-CT-300 at 7.5 % = 235.88;
now = 2026-10-15 09:00 KL.

## From the market research and the clarifications
| ID | Source | Layer | Input | Expected | What a broken implementation does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EC-17 | Duplicate customers [17] | U, C | ERPNext has "Tanjung Electrical Contractors Sdn Bhd". The CRM account is "TANJUNG ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS SDN BHD", or the same name padded with spaces | c2 uses the stripped name, and ERPNext matches it regardless of case (CT-8). The order uses ERPNext's `data.name`; no c3. Inner-whitespace differences are out of scope | The client lower-cases or compares in Python and creates a second customer; it sends the padded name (404); or it sends the CRM spelling as `customer` |
| EC-18 | Group record as customer group [4] | E, C | Customer Group "Commercial" and Territories "Malaysia"/"Singapore" | `is_group` 0 for each; live c3 returns 200 for both territories | The territory points at "All Territories" or a group, and c3 fails with "Cannot select a Group type…" on every new account |
| EC-19 | Tax added twice [7][16] | U, C | Malaysian order; template with 1 row | The body carries exactly the template's 1 row. | EC-20 | Silent sync failure [9][3] | U | c5 raises Refused("Could not find Item: FG-XX-999") on Won | In the same response: a warning flash with the reason; the deal panel shows a red "failed" chip with the reason; the deal is on My day's "ERP orders not created". No background retry (`fake.calls` has exactly one c5) | The error is only logged, the status is left as "not sent", or a hidden retry thread runs |
| EC-21 | Raw ERP errors [10] | U | 417 message `Item <b>FG-XX-999</b> &amp; 3 more: &lt;script&gt;x&lt;/script&gt;` | Stored text is `Item FG-XX-999 & 3 more: <script>x</script>`, rendered escaped on the page and on My day | Tags kept, or the unescaped text rendered with `|safe` (XSS) |
